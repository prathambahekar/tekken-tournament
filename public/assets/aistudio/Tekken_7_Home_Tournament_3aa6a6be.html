Rules

  • Match: the first player to win 2 rounds wins the match.
  • Points: a 2–0 win gives 3 points. A 2–1 win gives 2 points. The loser always gets 0.
  • Q and DQ: in the points table, Q means a player has already secured a top-4 spot and DQ means they can no longer reach it. Until that is certain, the rank number is shown.
  • Knockout: 1st vs 2nd gives W1 and L1. 3rd vs 4th gives W2 and L2.
  • Winners match: W1 vs W2. The winner goes to the final and the loser gets a second chance.
  • Losers match: L1 vs L2. The loser is out, and the winner plays the loser of the winners match. The winner of that match goes to the final.
  • On this page: click a player in the knockout to mark the winner, and click again to undo. Solid lines are winners and dashed lines are losers.
